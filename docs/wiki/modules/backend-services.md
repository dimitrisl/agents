# ⚙️ Subsystem: Backend Services (`backend/services/`)

## 📌 Overview
The `backend/services/` package houses the core business logic of Phyrexian Forge. Services encapsulate domain rules independently of HTTP frameworks and database drivers where possible.

---

## 🔑 Key Services

| Service | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| [`stats_service.py`](../../../backend/services/stats_service.py) | Mathematical calculation authority: calculates derived stats (`process_character_update`), AC breakdown (magic armor $+N$ & named magic armors *Elven Chain*/*Dwarven Plate*/*Dragon Scale Mail* plus `ignore_proficiency` auto-equip, `unarmored_only` gating for *Bracers of Defense* & *Robe of the Archmagi*, homebrew AC items, accurate shield classification via `_is_shield_item`, starting equipment auto-equip bounded strictly to initial sync, Monk unarmored preservation, 2024 *College of Dance* unarmored AC `10 + DEX + CHA` gated on Bard $\ge 3$, dynamic attunement slot fallback, attunement = attuned AND equipped, comma/parenthetical item lookup normalization, and `save_bonus` for *Cloak*/*Ring of Protection*, *Stone of Good Luck*, *Staff of Power*, *Robe of Stars*), Ability Check & Initiative bonuses (`ability_check_bonus` for *Stone of Good Luck*, `initiative_advantage` for *Sentinel Shield*/*Weapon of Warning*), Caster Focus modifiers (`spell_attack_bonus` & `spell_save_dc_bonus` for *Wand of the War Mage*, *Rod of the Pact Keeper*, *Amulet of the Devout*, *Robe of the Archmagi*, *Staff of Power*), `stat_max: 20` capped bonuses ordered before uncapped *Manuals*/*Tomes* and `stat_set` overrides, Saving Throws (normalized 3-letter `saving_throws`, Paladin L6+ *Aura of Protection*, Monk L14+ *Diamond Soul*, Rogue L15+ *Slippery Mind* (`WIS` in 2014, `WIS` + `CHA` in 2024), Artificer L20 *Soul of Artifice*), HP with retroactive CON adjustments (`con_mod_applied` initialized from innate `base_stats`), multiclass spell slots, unlocked subclass `bonus_spells` exemption from `max_spell_level` pruning, weapon proficiencies (dynamically re-evaluated, non-sticky) & `NONE` non-proficient mod + *Agonizing Blast* (`+CHA`) + *Bracers of Archery* (`+2` bow damage, non-stacking) + attack/damage calculations, weapon mastery Save DCs, `senses`/`damage_resistances`/`save_advantages` derivation (including *Umbral Sight* upgrade after *Eyes of the Dark* & unresolved Dragonborn/Tiefling ancestral placeholder suppression), 2024 *Divine Order: Thaumaturge* (`Arcana`/`Religion` $+\max(1, \text{WIS})$) & *Primal Order: Magician* (`Arcana`/`Nature` $+\max(1, \text{WIS})$) skill bonuses, edition-aware Exhaustion (`2024` `-2/lvl` D20 Tests & `-5 ft/lvl` speed vs. `2014` speed/HP halving via `base_hp_max`, Level 6 death `speed=0, hp_current=0, concentrating_on=None`, and `exhaustion_bypass`), non-destructive `magic_items_suppressed` (Athas/Antimagic), signed `dm_modifiers`, 2014 vs. 2024 Jack of All Trades / Remarkable Athlete splits, 2014-only *Observant* feat passive perception (+5), and High Elf / Wizard cantrip exclusion guard against false Eldritch Knight inference. |
| [`rest_service.py`](../../../backend/services/rest_service.py) | Deterministic short and long rest execution (`apply_short_rest` including 2014 Sorcerer L20 *Sorcerous Restoration* `+4 SP` exclusion of 2024, `apply_long_rest`), 1-shot DM rest unlock relocking, Exhaustion Level 6 death guards (`is_deceased_by_exhaustion`), and campaign-detach DM control cleanup (`dm_control_reset_fields`, `reset_campaign_dm_controls`). |
| [`class_resources_service.py`](../../../backend/services/class_resources_service.py) | Data-driven class & racial resource pools (`data/rules/class_resources.json`, 2014 & 2024 plus Dragonborn *Breath Weapon* edition scaling and Half-Orc/Orc *Relentless Endurance*) synced on every save: follows level-ups and multiclassing, adopts hand-made pools, preserves user `max_adjustment` across level-ups, and powers Sorcerer Flexible Casting & Metamagic. |
| [`validation_service.py`](../../../backend/services/validation_service.py) | Deterministic build validation and proficiency authority: verifies ability scores, armor/weapon proficiencies (primary class starting proficiencies + PHB Multiclassing proficiency tables `_MULTICLASS_ARMOR_PROFS_2014`/`_2024` & `_MULTICLASS_WEAPON_PROFS_2014`/`_2024` + subclass domains/Hexblade + 2024 *Divine Order: Protector* / *Primal Order: Warden* proficiency gating with combined-placeholder suppression + racial weapon grants + equipped magic item `weapon_proficiencies` such as *Bracers of Archery* + 2024 Rogue Finesse Martial / Monk Light Martial property checks via `get_character_weapon_proficiencies` & `is_weapon_proficient`), subclass progression levels, feat requirements, and duplicate non-repeatable feat detection/repair (`deterministic_validate_build`), plus shared helpers (`resolve_known_feat`, `get_owned_non_repeatable_feats`, `calculate_feature_hp_bonus_per_level`) used across stats and progression services. |
| [`forge_service.py`](../../../backend/services/forge_service.py) | Character creation & level-up engine: orchestrates prompt generation, AI character synthesis, portrait prompt generation, manual character enrichment, dynamic 2014/2024 subclass resolution & milestones (`get_subclass_entry`), feat prerequisite filtering (`Spellcasting`, armor/weapon profs, exact race boundaries, 2014 & 2024 `stat_requirements`), edition-aware 2014 & 2024 structured class choice pickers (`divine_order`, `primal_order`, `fighting_style`, `pact_boon`, `eldritch_invocation`, `metamagic`, `maneuver`, `infusion` `{2:4,6:2,10:2,14:2,18:2}`, `hunter_choice`), server-side allow-list & cardinality validators (`validate_manual_class_choices`, `validate_level_up_structured_choices`), `Resilient` saving throw proficiency sync across AI/Manual Forge and level-up, and deterministic injection of mandatory class, species, and background build grants via `inject_mandatory_character_grants`. |
| [`character_import_service.py`](../../../backend/services/character_import_service.py) | Parses and normalizes external PDF character sheets and JSON exports into validated `CharacterSchema` objects. |
| [`vtt_service.py`](../../../backend/services/vtt_service.py) | Virtual Tabletop engine: atomic token movement, token additions/removals, multi-scene management, Fog of War, grid configuration, and player state redaction (`redact_for_player`, stripping secret `is_hidden` tokens, Fog-hidden tokens, and `hp`/`max_hp` on visible `is_enemy` tokens). |
| [`session_service.py`](../../../backend/services/session_service.py) | Session recap engine: audio streaming to Gemini multimodal, transcription, entity extraction (NPCs, locations, loot), and text session processing. |
| [`rules_service.py`](../../../backend/services/rules_service.py) | Deterministic mandatory build grant injection (`inject_mandatory_character_grants` including `grant_subclass_bonus_spells` and structured innate racial spells), `autofix_character_build`, subclass progression scaling resolution, 2014/2024 class matrix evaluation, rules comparison engine, and PDF character extraction parser. |
| [`homebrew_service.py`](../../../backend/services/homebrew_service.py) | CRUD operations, AI forge generation, and JSON export/import for custom campaign homebrew items. |
| [`dm_service.py`](../../../backend/services/dm_service.py) & [`encounter_service.py`](../../../backend/services/encounter_service.py) | AI Dungeon Master assistant: dynamic encounter balancing against actual party levels, tactical monster briefs, on-the-fly NPC generation, and session prep tools. |
| [`campaign_service.py`](../../../backend/services/campaign_service.py) & [`user_admin_service.py`](../../../backend/services/user_admin_service.py) | Campaign lifecycle helpers (`build_party_update_payload` for canonical WS payloads, cascading campaign deletion resetting DM controls on detached heroes) and administrative user management (pagination, role changes, cascading cleanup). |
| [`dice_service.py`](../../../backend/services/dice_service.py) & [`progression_service.py`](../../../backend/services/progression_service.py) | Server-side dice roll validation/dispatch with computational DoS limits (caps dice count $\le 100$, sides $\le 1000$, total dice $\le 200$) and character level progression helpers. |
| [`module_parser_service.py`](../../../backend/services/module_parser_service.py) | Ingests D&D adventure modules (PDF/Markdown), extracts maps, handouts, and encounters, and cleans temporary assets older than 24h. |
