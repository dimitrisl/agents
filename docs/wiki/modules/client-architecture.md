# 🖥️ Subsystem: Frontend Architecture, DM Workspace & Forge (`client/src/app/`)

## 📌 Overview
The Angular 19 frontend uses a standalone component architecture powered by **Angular Signals** for reactive state, pure TypeScript rule helpers in `core/rules/`, a custom dark "Phyrexian" UI component library in `shared/ui/`, and real-time WebSocket synchronization.

> [!NOTE]
> For detailed documentation on the **Player Sheet & Companion (`client/src/app/features/player/`)**, see **[Player Companion & Character Sheet](client-player.md)**.

### 2. Pure Client Rules Engine (`core/rules/`)
Pure, deterministic TypeScript functions tested in isolation that mirror `backend/services/stats_service.py`:
- [`ability.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/ability.ts), [`hit-points.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/hit-points.ts), [`skills.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/skills.ts): Ability modifiers, edition detection (`is2024Edition`), `effectiveExhaustionLevel`, `isDeceasedByExhaustion`, `exhaustionD20Penalty`, `hasExhaustionDisadvantage`, `resolveRollMode` (5e Adv/Dis cancellation), `maxAttunementSlots` (Artificer L10/14/18 + features), `equipmentAbilityCheckBonus` & `abilityCheckModifier` (mirroring *Stone of Good Luck / Luckstone* under `!magic_items_suppressed`), `hasInitiativeAdvantage` (*Sentinel Shield* & *Weapon of Warning* within attunement cap), normalized `isSaveProficient` + `savingThrowModifier` (mirroring *Diamond Soul*, *Slippery Mind* WIS + 2024 CHA, *Aura of Protection*, *Soul of Artifice* + *Cloak/Ring of Protection*, *Stone of Good Luck*, *Staff of Power*, *Robe of Stars* gated on `!magic_items_suppressed` with comma/parenthetical catalog normalization), `hasReliableTalent` (2014 Rogue L11 vs 2024 Rogue L7), `orderSkillBonus` (2024 Cleric *Thaumaturge* +WIS on Arcana/Religion & Druid *Magician* +WIS on Arcana/Nature), `passivePerceptionOf`, `passiveInvestigationOf`, `passiveInsightOf`, `describeDmModifiers`, proficiency bonuses, multiclass class/subclass summaries (`formatClassSummary`, `formatSubclassSummary`), HP bounds, and passive scores.
- [`spell-casting.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/spell-casting.ts) & [`spell-slots.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/spell-slots.ts): Upcast damage/healing dice scaling, `analyzeSpell()` ritual tag parsing, `RITUAL_SPELLS_2014`/`RITUAL_SPELLS_2024`, `maxPreparedSpells()`, `preparedSpellsCount()` (excluding cantrips & always-prepared spells), `isAlwaysPreparedSpell()`, 2014 vs 2024 `canCastAsRitual()`, spell attack crits, and slot consumption/recovery (including synchronized `pact.used` tracking).
- [`flexible-casting.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/flexible-casting.ts) & [`metamagic.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/metamagic.ts): Sorcerer spell slot $\leftrightarrow$ Sorcery Point conversions and Metamagic cost/effect rules.
- [`weapon-calc.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/weapon-calc.ts) & [`weapon-masteries.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/weapon-masteries.ts): Weapon to-hit/damage calculation supporting non-destructive `magic_items_suppressed`, signed `dm_modifiers`, `getAgonizingBlastBonus` (`+CHA` without double-counting), `getBracersOfArcheryBonus` (`+2` bow damage), normalized `weapon.properties` (`string | string[]`), conditional weapon effects (e.g., *vs undead*, *vs fiend*), and 2024 mastery triggers.
- [`encumbrance.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/encumbrance.ts): Pure TS inventory weight parser (`parseWeightLb`, catalog weight lookup matching `items.json` & `weapons.json`), coin weight ($50\text{ coins}=1\text{ lb}$), `computeCarryingCapacity` / `computeEncumbranceSummary` (2014/2024 *Powerful Build*/*Brawny* & *Aspect of the Beast: Bear* parity), ammunition classification & half-recovery (`classifyWeaponAmmo`, `recoverExpendedAmmo`), and utility consumable classifiers (`classifyUtilityConsumable`).
- [`rest.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/rest.ts), [`class-resources.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/class-resources.ts), [`level-up.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/level-up.ts), [`recommendations.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/recommendations.ts): Short/Long Rest recovery rules (including multiclass hit die resolution `hitDieSize` / `availableHitDieOptions`, 2014 Sorcerer L20 *Sorcerous Restoration* `+4 SP` across `Sorcery Points`/`Sorcery Point`/`Font of Magic`, 2014 Bard `songOfRestDie` scaling `1d6`–`1d12`, `shortRestRecoveryBudget` / `harnessDivinePowerMaxSlotLevel` / `hasShortRestSlotRecovery` / `applyShortRestSlotRecovery`, `0`-Hit-Dice short rests when slot recovery is available, and class resource pool resets), immutable class resource CRUD helpers (`upsertClassResourceList`, `setClassResourceCurrent`, `removeClassResourceAt`), level-up progression & multiclass eligibility helpers (`ALL_DND_CLASSES`, `getMulticlassGrantedProficiencies`, `getMulticlassEligibility` with 2014 vs 2024 parity), and Background/Species mandatory grants (`getBackgroundSkills`, `getBackgroundOriginFeat`, `getRacialSkills`, `getRacialBonusSkillCount`, `getMaxOriginFeats`).

## 📐 Frontend Invariants
1. **Prevent `409 Conflict` on Saves:** Always update `CharacterStateService` from the HTTP response of `PUT /characters/{id}` (`tap(updated => ...)`), which carries the incremented `version` from the backend. Never fire concurrent uncoordinated full-sheet `PUT` requests with a stale `version`.
2. **No Polling for Live Campaign State:** Never poll REST endpoints for party HP, initiative, whispers, or VTT state; subscribe to `WebSocketService` streams.
3. **Pure Rules in `core/rules/`:** Keep mechanical math in `client/src/app/core/rules/` free of Angular DI or DOM dependencies so it remains unit-testable and deterministic (including 2024 `Artificer` prepared spells, `"Human (2024)"` origin feat count excluding half-races, multiclass `classes` feat summation, `Dart` finesse/global bonuses, and `char.skills` authority).
4. **Resilient Auth, Signal Isolation, Campaign Switch Guards & UI Primitives:** [`AuthService`](../../../client/src/app/core/services/auth.service.ts) preserves cached user sessions across transient 5xx/network outages while failing closed on 401/403 alongside [`authGuard`](../../../client/src/app/core/guards/auth.guard.ts). [`CharacterStateService`](../../../client/src/app/core/services/character-state.service.ts) reacts to edition changes on `[data-edition]` and clears active hero storage on `reset()`. [`HomebrewService`](../../../client/src/app/core/services/homebrew.service.ts) catches trailing `loadHomebrew` GET errors after write operations so committed mutations stay successful. [`NotesPanelComponent`](../../../client/src/app/features/dm/panels/notes-panel/notes-panel.component.ts) cancels pending 1500 ms debounced saves on `@Input() campaignName` change while [`DmComponent.onCampaignSelect()`](../../../client/src/app/features/dm/dm.component.ts) flushes pending drafts explicitly to the prior campaign. [`WebSocketService`](../../../client/src/app/core/services/websocket.service.ts) returns `boolean` send status and shows a rate-limited offline toast on failed non-ping transmits. Interactive forms/prompts/cards must follow [`design-contract.md`](../../../client/design-contract.md) using [`shared/ui`](../../../client/src/app/shared/ui/index.ts) directives/modals (`forgeInput`, `forgeSelect`, `forgeTextarea`, `<forge-modal>`, `[loading]`, `bg-panel` inside modals, max one `variant="primary"` per view, `ForgeConfirmService.askPrompt`) and theme tokens (`--theme-accent`, `red`, `gold`, `emerald`, `cobalt`).
5. **Forge Edition Parity & Model Defaults:** [`ForgeComponent`](../../../client/src/app/features/forge/forge.component.ts) scopes `raceOptions` (excluding 2024-only species such as `Warforged` from 2014 PHB selections while exposing full 2014 PHB races/subraces: `Variant Human`, `High Elf`, `Wood Elf`, `Dark Elf (Drow)`, `Half-Elf`, `Hill Dwarf`, `Mountain Dwarf`, `Lightfoot Halfling`, `Stout Halfling`, `Forest Gnome`, and `Rock Gnome`) and `bgOptions` (`backgrounds_2024.json` vs `2014`) to the active ruleset, resets incompatible `aiRace`/`aiBackground` selections to `'AI Choice'` inside `toggleEditionAndRefresh()` and `restoreAiDraft()`, and pre-fills Step 4 Origin Ability Adjustments from `RACE_2014_PRESETS` (including `Mountain Dwarf` `+2 CON / +2 STR` and automatic `+1` to all six stats for 2014 `Human` in `getFinalStat()`) / `BG_2024_PRESETS`. [`SettingsComponent`](../../../client/src/app/features/settings/settings.component.ts) aligns `DEFAULT_MODEL_OPTIONS` and `preferredModel` (`gemini-3.5-flash`) with [`config_loader.py`](../../../backend/core/config_loader.py).
