# ⚙️ Concept: Deterministic Rules Engine vs. AI (`docs/wiki/concepts/rules-engine.md`)

## 📌 Philosophy: The "Bio-Mechanical" Boundary
In **Phyrexian Forge**, game mechanics and AI are strictly decoupled:
* **The "Mechanical" Core (Deterministic):** All game rules, stat derivations, level-ups, combat modifiers, spell slot progressions, and legality checks are executed strictly through deterministic Python algorithms reading from static JSON datasets.
* **The "Biological" Shell (AI Generative):** Large Language Models (Gemini) are used solely for creative and unstructured tasks: character backstories, roleplay advice, session narrative summarization, and natural language query interpretation.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Zero Hallucination Guarantee:** Never allow an LLM to compute Armor Class, Hit Points, Saving Throw DCs, or determine legal spell slots.

---

## 🏛️ Calculation Authority: `stats_service.py`
[`backend/services/stats_service.py`](../../../backend/services/stats_service.py) is the sole authority for all derived character statistics:

1. **Armor Class (AC) Breakdown & Equipment Invariants:**
   - Computes base AC from equipped armor (Light: Base + Full DEX; Medium: Base + min(DEX, 2); Heavy: Base, no DEX).
   - **Shield Detection & Classification Invariant (`_is_shield_item`):**
     - Recognizes shields by checking catalog item type `shield` or canonical shield names (`"shield"`, `"wooden shield"`, `"steel shield"`, `"buckler"`, `"tower shield"`, or `r"^shield(\s*,\s*|\s+)\+[1-3]$"`).
     - Explicitly excludes wondrous accessories, rings, amulets, and cloaks (e.g. *Brooch of Shielding*) to prevent granting an illegitimate +2 shield bonus or disabling Monk/Barbarian Unarmored Defense.
     - Enforces the shield limit: maximum of **one shield** bonus (+2 AC) applies even if multiple shields are present in equipment.
   - **Starting Equipment Auto-Equip Invariant (`DEF-CR-02`):**
     - Starting equipment auto-equip executes **only** when `(is_initial_sync or has_unassigned_equipment)` (e.g., initial character creation where `base_stats` is unset or equipment has no `equipped` key).
     - **Player Intent Preservation:** When an existing character explicitly unequips an item (`equipped: False`), future saves, rests, or syncs **never** re-equip it automatically.
     - **Unarmored Defense Protection:** Monks (`char_class == "monk"`) are strictly excluded from armor and shield auto-equip to preserve their Unarmored Defense.
   - **Attunement Slots Fallback:** Defaults dynamically to 3 attunement slots, scaling to 4, 5, or 6 if Artificer subclass/class features (*Magic Item Adept*, *Magic Item Savant*, *Magic Item Master*) are present.
   - Enforces Defense Fighting Style requirement: requires wearing armor (does not apply unarmored).
   - **Heavy Armor Strength Thresholds (`min_str` & `heavy_armor_speed_penalty`):** Equipped heavy armors in `data/rules/items.json` (*Ring Mail* 0, *Chain Mail* 13, *Splint*/*Plate Armor* 15) are evaluated in [`validation_service.py`](../../../backend/services/validation_service.py) (`get_heavy_armor_strength_penalty`) against synced effective STR (so *Gauntlets of Ogre Power* or *Belt of Giant Strength* qualify). Catalog Medium/Light armor (*Breastplate*, *Half Plate*, etc.) is explicitly excluded even for `+X` variants. Missing the threshold applies `-10 ft` Walking Speed (`heavy_armor_speed_penalty`) unless exempt (2014 *Dwarf*, *Mithral* armor, Armorer Artificer *Arcane Armor*, or equipped *Boots of Striding and Springing*) and surfaces an informational notice via [`collect_build_suggestions`](../../../backend/services/validation_service.py) across both [`POST /rules/validate`](../../../server/routers/rules_router.py) and `autofix_character_build` without failing validation.
   - Returns both the total AC integer and an `ACBreakdown` object detailing exact mathematical components for player popovers.

2. **Retroactive Constitution Adjustments:**
   - Adjustments to Constitution score (e.g. from level-ups, ASI, or stat overrides) immediately and retroactively update both `hp_max` and `hp_current` (+1 HP per level per +1 CON mod).

3. **Multiclass Spell Slots, Warlock Pact Magic & Prepared/Ritual Rules (`#261`):**
   - Multiclass caster levels follow the official multiclassing table (Full Casters 1:1, Half Casters 1:2, Third Casters 1:3), with `total_caster_level > 20` clamped to the level 20 table in `calculate_max_spell_slots`.
   - Warlock **Pact Magic** slots are tracked separately from standard spellcasting slots; Short Rest restores only Pact Magic slots, while regular slots require a Long Rest.
   - **Prepared Spells Cap & Always-Prepared Separation (`#261`):** `calculate_max_prepared_spells()` derives `max_prepared_spells` (`None` for 2014 Known casters *Bard*, *Sorcerer*, *Warlock*, *Ranger*, *Eldritch Knight*, *Arcane Trickster*, Paladin L1; `Level + Mod` or $\lfloor \text{Level}/2 \rfloor + \text{Mod}$ for 2014 Prepared casters; `PREPARED_2024_TABLES` across full/half/third/2024 Artificer casters in 2024 Revision, summed across multiclass classes), `cap_prepared_leveled_spells()` enforces that cap on Forge/Autofix leveled spells while keeping cantrips and always-prepared grants intact, and `collect_always_prepared_spells()` extracts unlocked subclass `bonus_spells`, species `innate_spells`/`lineage_spells`, and 2024 base-class always-prepared spells (*Hunter's Mark* Ranger L1, *Divine Smite* Paladin L2, *Find Steed* Paladin L5, *Words of Creation* Bard L20; also seeded into `spells` by `inject_mandatory_character_grants()`) into `always_prepared_spells` ([`pdf_exporter.py`](../../../backend/utils/pdf_exporter.py) evaluates `manual_prepared = prepared_spells - always_prepared_spells` alongside `always_prepared_spells` so 2014 Known casters export all known spells checked, while `hydrate_character_spellcasting_and_resources()` hydrates legacy DB docs on `GET /characters` and `rest_service.py` hydrates missing *Arcane/Natural Recovery* pools before short-rest checks).
   - **Ritual Casting (`2014` vs `2024`):** `spells_2014.json` (`Purify Food and Drink: true`, `Hold Person: false`) and `spells_2024.json` drive `canCastAsRitual()` in [`spell-casting.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/spell-casting.ts) with eponymous SRD prefix normalization (*Leomund's Tiny Hut*, etc.): 2014 Wizard from spellbook, 2014 Bard from known spells, 2014 Cleric/Druid/Artificer only when prepared, plus *Ritual Caster* / *Book of Ancient Secrets* (whereas 2014 *Pact of the Tome* without *Book of Ancient Secrets* does not unlock ritual casting); 2024 Revision allows any prepared ritual caster plus Wizard *Ritual Adept* / *Pact of the Tome* / feat book casters.

4. **Rest Recovery Invariants (`client/src/app/core/rules/rest.ts`):**
   - **Long Rest Hit Dice Recovery:**
     - **2024 Revision (PHB 2024, p. 370):** A character regains all lost HP and **all spent Hit Point Dice** (`hitDiceRecovered = totalHitDice`, `hitDiceUsed = 0`).
     - **2014 Edition (PHB 2014, p. 186):** A character regains up to half of their total Hit Dice floored (`Math.floor(totalHitDice / 2) || 1`).
     - **Edition Detection:** Evaluated via `is2024Edition(char)`, inspecting both `char.dnd_edition` and `char.ruleset_version`.
   - **Short Rest Resource Resets, Spell Slot Recovery & Bard Song of Rest:**
     - 2024 rules recover +1 expended use on Short Rest for *Rage*, *Second Wind*, *Channel Divinity*, and *Wild Shape*.
     - **2014 Edition Sorcerer L20 (*Sorcerous Restoration*):** Recovers up to `+4` expended *Sorcery Points* (capped at `max`) on Short Rest in [`rest_service.py`](../../../backend/services/rest_service.py) and [`rest.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/rest.ts); excluded cleanly when `"2024" in edition`.
     - **Short-Rest Spell Slot Recovery (`#261`):** [`apply_short_rest`](../../../backend/services/rest_service.py) (`POST /characters/{id}/short-rest`), [`class_resources_service.py`](../../../backend/services/class_resources_service.py), and [`rest.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/rest.ts) validate and apply **Arcane Recovery** (Wizard, $\lceil \text{WizLvl}/2 \rceil$ combined levels of 1st–5th level slots, `1/Long Rest`), **Natural Recovery** (Circle of the Land Druid: L2+ in `2014`, L6+ in `2024`, $\lceil \text{DruidLvl}/2 \rceil$ levels, `1/Long Rest`), and **Harness Divine Power** (Cleric L2+ / Paladin L3+, 1 slot $\le \lceil \text{PB}/2 \rceil$, pre-checking `Channel Divinity >= 1` before short-rest recharge and decrementing 1 use). Zero-Hit-Dice short rests are permitted whenever Pact Magic or slot recovery is available.
     - **2014 Edition Bard (*Song of Rest*):** Scales extra short-rest Hit Die healing deterministically across L2 (`1d6`), L9 (`1d8`), L13 (`1d10`), and L17 (`1d12`) via [`songOfRestDie`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/rest.ts) (returns `null` in 2024).
     - **Racial & Warlock Resource Pools:** [`class_resources_service.py`](../../../backend/services/class_resources_service.py) tracks *Dragonborn Breath Weapon* (`2014`: 1/Short Rest; `2024`: $\text{PB}$/Long Rest), *Half-Orc* / *Orc* *Relentless Endurance* (1/Long Rest), and 2014 Warlock L20 *Eldritch Master* (1/Long Rest).

5. **2024 Weapon Masteries, Unarmored Formulas & Order Choices:**
   - Limits weapon masteries known based on 2024 class tables (Paladin/Ranger/Rogue: 2; Barbarian: 2–4; Fighter: 3–6), summing across all martial classes in `classes` for multiclass builds ([`stats_service.py`](../../../backend/services/stats_service.py)). Existing masteries that already match equipped weapons are preserved in order before appending newly equipped or default fallback masteries. Canonical 2024 mastery descriptions from `data/rules/weapon_masteries_2024.json` are served via `GET /api/v1/rules/weapon-masteries`.
   - Calculates dynamic Topple Save DC: `8 + Proficiency Bonus + weapon scaling ability modifier` (via `resolveWeaponStat()`, supporting CHA/INT/WIS pact/blade weapons in addition to STR).
   - **Draconic Resilience & College of Dance (*Dazzling Footwork*):** In 2024 Revision, *Draconic Resilience* base unarmored AC is `10 + DEX + CHA` (no `13` floor) and *College of Dance* Bard (Bard level $\ge 3$, including multiclass builds) grants `10 + DEX + CHA` when unarmored and without a shield; Draconic HP bonus scales strictly with Sorcerer class levels in both [`stats_service.py`](../../../backend/services/stats_service.py) and [`progression_service.py`](../../../backend/services/progression_service.py), rather than total multiclass level.
   - **2024 Cleric *Divine Order* & Druid *Primal Order*:** [`validation_service.py`](../../../backend/services/validation_service.py) ignores generic/combined `"Divine Order"` / `"Primal Order"` placeholder prose and grants Heavy Armor + Martial Weapons exclusively under 2024 rules for `Divine Order: Protector` and Medium Armor + Martial Weapons for `Primal Order: Warden`. [`stats_service.py`](../../../backend/services/stats_service.py) and [`skills.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/skills.ts) (`orderSkillBonus`) add $+\max(1, \text{WIS mod})$ to `Arcana` & `Religion` for `Divine Order: Thaumaturge` and to `Arcana` & `Nature` for `Primal Order: Magician` strictly under 2024 rules, while [`rules_service.py`](../../../backend/services/rules_service.py), [`forge_service.py`](../../../backend/services/forge_service.py), and [`recommendations.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/recommendations.ts) (`getMaxCantrips`) grant `+1` cantrip in both backend and frontend. [`validate_manual_class_choices`](../../../backend/services/forge_service.py) strictly allow-lists `chosen_class_choices` by class, level, subclass, edition, cardinality, and prerequisites (rejecting spoofed or conflicting picks with `HTTP 400`).
   - **Unified JSON-First Subclass Feature Resolution:** [`resolve_level_up_automatic_features`](../../../backend/services/forge_service.py) reads `data/rules/subclasses/<edition>/<class>.json` first across both 2014 and 2024 editions, falling back to `SUBCLASS_FEATURES_DETAILS_2024` / `SUBCLASS_FEATURES_DETAILS` only when absent.
   - **Populated Spells Check (`_has_populated_spells` / `_has_populated_spell_slots`):** Fighter `Eldritch Knight` inference in [`stats_service.py`](../../../backend/services/stats_service.py) inspects the Fighter class's own level (`fighter_level >= 3`, never total multiclass level) and ignores empty `SpellsSchema` dicts; it never overrides an explicit Fighter subclass (from `classes` or top-level `subclass`) or multiclass characters that already include a spellcasting class.

6. **Weapon Attack & Damage Mathematical Breakdown (incl. Monk Martial Arts):**
   - **Attack Bonus:** `Proficiency Bonus (if proficient) + Ability Mod (STR, Finesse/Ranged DEX, or Monk Martial Arts max(STR, DEX) when unarmored without shield on Unarmed Strikes / shortswords / non-heavy non-two-handed simple melee weapons) + Magic Bonus (+1/+2/+3) + Fighting Style (Archery +2)`.
   - **Damage Roll Breakdown:** Returns an itemized structure including base weapon damage dice (automatically upgrading Monk weapons & Unarmed Strikes up to the edition Martial Arts die: `1d4`–`1d10` in 2014, `1d6`–`1d12` in 2024), ability score modifier, magical bonus, and conditional fighting style additions (e.g., Dueling +2 when wielding a melee weapon in one hand with no other weapons).
   - **Class Speed Scaling:** [`sync_character_stats`](../../../backend/services/stats_service.py) also applies **Monk Unarmored Movement** (`+10` to `+30 ft` when unarmored and without a shield at L2/6/10/14/18) and **Barbarian Fast Movement** (`+10 ft` at L5+ when not wearing Heavy Armor).

7. **Subclass Feature Resolution & Dynamic Scaling:**
   - Ingests class matrices from `data/rules/classes.json` and evaluates level gates dynamically.
   - **Horizon Walker Ranger:**
     - *Planar Warrior:* Base 1d8 force damage on weapon hit, automatically scaling to **2d8** at Ranger Level 11.
     - *Distant Strike (Level 11):* Resolves teleportation feature (up to 10 feet before each attack) and enables a 3rd attack against a distinct target.
     - *Spectral Defense (Level 15):* Reaction ability granting resistance against all damage dealt by the incoming attack.
   - Enforces backwards and forward compatibility between 2014 and 2024 rulesets.

8. **Interactive Skills, Expertise & Passive Scores:**
   - Skills support three distinct states: **Normal** (no bonus), **Proficient** (`+PB`), and **Expertise** (`+2 * PB`).
   - Toggling state recalculates the total skill check modifier dynamically: `AbilityMod + (PB * Multiplier)`.
   - Passive perception, passive insight, and passive investigation update automatically based on active proficiency/expertise:
     - Passive Perception: `10 + WIS mod + (PB if proficient, 2*PB if expertise, or half_prof if Jack of All Trades) + bonus_modifier`.
     - Automatically integrates flat feat bonuses (e.g. **+5** from the 2014 *Observant* feat) and custom `passive_perception_bonus`.
     - Passive Insight: `10 + WIS mod + (PB if proficient, 2*PB if expertise)`.
     - Passive Investigation: `10 + INT mod + (PB if proficient, 2*PB if expertise)`.

9. **2014 vs. 2024 Edition Split (Jack of All Trades & Remarkable Athlete):**
   - **Jack of All Trades (Bard Level 2):**
     - Both 2014 & 2024 RAW: Applies $\lfloor PB / 2 \rfloor$ to all ability checks lacking proficiency.
   - **Remarkable Athlete (Champion Fighter Level 7):**
     - **2014 RAW:** Applies $\lceil PB / 2 \rceil$ (half PB rounded up) to physical ability checks (Strength, Dexterity, Constitution) lacking proficiency. This applies to physical skill checks (Athletics, Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, Stealth) and to Initiative (`DEX mod + ceil(PB / 2)`).
     - **2024 RAW:** Grants **Advantage** on Initiative checks and Athletics checks, with no numeric modifier bonus.

10. **Dynamic Advantage State Hygiene:**
    - Evaluates boolean advantage flags (`initiative_advantage`, `athletics_advantage`) dynamically on each sync cycle from subclass features (e.g. 2024 Remarkable Athlete, Barbarian *Feral Instinct*) and equipped magic items (e.g. *Weapon of Warning*).
    - Replaces sticky `setdefault(..., False)` logic to guarantee that changing subclasses or unequipping items cleanly clears active advantage flags.

11. **Combat Action Incapacitation Guard (`DEF-CR-01`):**
    - Checked via [`isIncapacitated(char)`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/hit-points.ts#L22-L35).
    - Pristine characters (`hp_current: undefined / None`) resolve effective HP via `currentHp(char)` to `char.hp_max > 0` and are **not** incapacitated.
    - Characters are blocked from combat actions (Dodge, Dash, Disengage, Help) **only** when `currentHp(char) <= 0` or when affected by active incapacitating conditions (`incapacitated`, `unconscious`, `paralyzed`, `petrified`, `stunned`).

12. **Deterministic Mandatory Build Grants (`inject_mandatory_character_grants`):**
    - Executed in [`backend/services/rules_service.py`](../../../backend/services/rules_service.py) across `forge_character`, `forge_character_manual`, and `autofix_character_build`:
    - **Species / Racial Grants:** Automatically injects all static species traits from `races_{edition}.json` (`source: "Race"`), racial skill proficiencies (*Keen Senses* $\rightarrow$ `Perception`, 2024 *Naturally Stealthy* $\rightarrow$ `Stealth` without bleeding onto 2014 *Lightfoot Halfling*, 2014 *Menacing* $\rightarrow$ `Intimidation`), 2014 PHB subraces (`High Elf`, `Wood Elf` 35 ft, `Dark Elf (Drow)` with `"Drow"` alias, `Hill Dwarf` *Dwarven Toughness* +1 HP/lvl, `Mountain Dwarf` *Dwarven Armor Training*, `Lightfoot`/`Stout Halfling`, `Forest`/`Rock Gnome`, `Half-Elf` +2 CHA/+1/+1, `Variant Human` +1/+1, bonus skill, and L1 Feat), and racial base speeds (`35 ft` for Goliath/Wood Elf, `25 ft` for 2014 Dwarf/Halfling/Gnome).
    - **Background Grants:** Automatically merges mandatory background skill proficiencies (`backgrounds_{edition}.json`) into `skill_proficiencies`, injects background tool proficiencies and 2014 background features (`source: "Background"`), and injects the 2024 Background Origin Feat (plus the 2nd Origin Feat for 2024 Humans via *Versatile*) into `features_traits` and `advancements`.
    - **Class & Starting Loadout Fallback:** Deterministically injects all class and active subclass features up to each multiclass entry's own level (`classes` array, falling back to `char_class`/`char_level` when empty) via [`resolve_level_up_automatic_features`](../../../backend/services/forge_service.py) (replacing generic placeholders such as `"Sorcerer Subclass"` or `"Subclass Feature"` whenever a concrete subclass is selected across `forge_character`, `forge_character_manual`, and `inject_mandatory_character_grants`), assigns class-prioritized Standard Array ability scores on offline AI fallback, and populates canonical starting weapons, armor/equipment, languages, and unlocked higher-tier spells & cantrips (`level_1`..`max_slot_lvl` when fallback/high-level characters lack unlocked tiers).
    - **PDF Import Feat HP Preservation:** [`parse_character_from_text`](../../../backend/services/rules_service.py) includes `calculate_feature_flat_hp_bonus` alongside `hp_bonus_per_level` when validating imported `hp_max` bounds and initializes `feat_hp_bonus` so pre-baked feats such as *Boon of Fortitude* (`+40 HP`) are neither stripped nor double-counted during `sync_character_stats()`.

13. **Exhaustion (`exhaustion_level`), Bypass, Level 6 Death, Athas Magic Suppression & Signed DM Modifiers:**
    - `exhaustion_level` is clamped to `[0, 6]` in [`stats_service.py`](../../../backend/services/stats_service.py) (`sync_character_stats`) and mirrored in [`ability.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/ability.ts), [`skills.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/skills.ts), [`weapon-calc.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/weapon-calc.ts), and [`rest.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/rest.ts):
      - **2024 Revision (5.5e):** Applies $-5 \times \text{level}$ ft speed (reaching `0` at L6) and $-2 \times \text{level}$ penalty to **D20 Tests only** (ability/skill checks, saving throws, Passive Perception, Initiative, weapon attacks, spell attacks); `spell_save_dc` is NOT penalized. Custom weapons (`is_custom: true`) track applied deltas via `exhaustion_attack_penalty_applied` so repeated saves never compound-decay.
      - **2014 Edition:** Applies L1+ Disadvantage on Ability Checks, Skills, and Initiative (subtracting `5` from Passive Perception), half speed at L2+, L3+ Disadvantage on Saving Throws, Death Saves, Concentration, and Attacks, halves `hp_max` at L4+ (preserving full ceiling in `base_hp_max` for lossless restoration), and speed `0` at L5+. Client rolls in `PlayerComponent`, `CombatPanelComponent`, and `DmComponent` (and server secret rolls via `_roll_in_secret`) apply 5e Advantage/Disadvantage cancellation (`resolveRollMode`).
      - **Level 6 Death & DM Bypass (`exhaustion_bypass`):** At effective exhaustion level `6` (unless `exhaustion_bypass == True`), `sync_character_stats()` enforces `speed = 0`, `hp_current = 0`, and `concentrating_on = None`; both client controls (`HitPointsControlComponent`, `ActionDockComponent`, `PlayerComponent`) and server endpoints (`/short-rest`, `/long-rest`, `/forge/level-up-apply`, DM party long rest) block healing, resting, and leveling. Toggling `exhaustion_bypass = True` retains the visible `exhaustion_level` badge while waiving penalties, Disadvantage, and Level 6 death.
      - **Athas / Antimagic Non-Destructive Suppression (`magic_items_suppressed`):** When enabled, `stats_service.py`, `validation_service.py`, and `weapon-calc.ts` preserve all items, custom gear, `EquipmentItem` custom bonus fields (`save_bonus`, `ability_check_bonus`, `spell_attack_bonus`, `spell_save_dc_bonus`, `bow_damage_bonus`), and `magic_bonus` fields in storage while suspending weapon enchantment `+X`, magic Armor/Shield `+X` bonuses (retaining mundane Plate `18` / Shield `+2` / physical *Sentinel Shield* `+2`), `Cloak`/`Ring of Protection` (`ac_bonus` and `save_bonus`), *Bracers of Defense* & *Robe of the Archmagi* (`unarmored_only`), *Stone of Good Luck* (`ability_check_bonus` & `save_bonus`), Caster Foci (`spell_attack_bonus` / `spell_save_dc_bonus`), *Bracers of Archery* (proficiency & `+2` bow damage), `stat_set`/`stat_bonus`/`mod1`/`mod2` items (applying `stat_max: 20`-capped *Ioun Stones*/*Belt of Dwarvenkind* before uncapped *Manuals*/*Tomes* and `stat_set` overrides, and cascading STR Heavy Armor speed penalties & CON HP Max changes), *Boots of Striding and Springing* Heavy Armor STR-penalty exemption (whereas non-magical *Mithral* stays exempt), and *Sentinel Shield* / *Weapon of Warning* initiative advantage. Custom weapons track suppressed magic delta via `magic_bonus_suppressed_applied`.
      - **Signed Custom DM Modifiers (`dm_modifiers`):** Ten idempotent signed integer fields (`attack_bonus`, `damage_bonus`, `ac_bonus`, `speed_bonus`, `hp_max_bonus`, `initiative_bonus`, `saving_throws_bonus`, `ability_checks_bonus`, `spell_save_dc_bonus`, `passive_perception_bonus`) plus `note` applied in `stats_service.py` (via `apply_character_weapon_modifiers`, `restore_base_hp_max` using separate `base_hp_max` for post-DM L4 halving and `pre_dm_hp_max` for `-hp_max_bonus` `1 HP` clamping, and level-up restoration in `forge_router.py`), `campaign_router.py` (`_SYNCED_CHAR_FIELDS`, `_sheet_modifier`, loot weapon distribution, and CAS updates), and `client/src/app/core/rules/` (`dm_hp_bonus_applied`, `dm_attack_bonus_applied`, `dm_damage_bonus_applied`, and `magic_bonus_suppressed_applied` mirrored in `CombatPanelComponent.syncWeaponCalculations`) to guarantee zero double-counting across repeated syncs and multi-tab `source: "player"` updates (`mergePartyUpdateFields`). Also `apply_short_rest` resets `death_saves` when healing a `0 HP` hero above `0`.
    - **Level 19+ 2024 Epic Boons & ASI Cap (`stat_cap = 30`):** [`feats_2024.json`](../../../data/rules/feats_2024.json) includes all 12 official 2024 PHB Epic Boons gated by `prerequisites.min_level = 19` (`stat_choice_options` across all 6 abilities, ignored in 2014 Edition and levels `< 19`). [`POST /forge/level-up-apply`](../../../server/routers/forge_router.py) and `analyze_level_up` enforce `stat_cap = 30` when `char_level >= 19` and `"2024" in dnd_edition` (else `20`), while [`validation_service.py`](../../../backend/services/validation_service.py) and [`stats_service.py`](../../../backend/services/stats_service.py) apply unique feat bonuses including `flat_hp_bonus` (*Boon of Fortitude*: `+40 HP`) and `speed_bonus` (*Boon of Speed*: `+30 ft`).

14. **Zero-LLM-Math & Level-Up Authority:**

## 🛡️ Deterministic Validation: `validation_service.py`
[`backend/services/validation_service.py`](../../../backend/services/validation_service.py) enforces build legality:
* **Armor & Weapon Proficiencies:** Validates that equipped items match character proficiencies fetched from `RulesRepository`.
* **Multiclassing `13+` Ability Prerequisites & PHB Level 1 Grants (`#264`):** [`validation_service.py`](../../../backend/services/validation_service.py) (`get_multiclass_eligibility`, `validate_multiclass_target`, `check_class_multiclass_prereq`, `get_multiclass_granted_proficiencies`, mirrored client-side in [`level-up.ts`](../../../client/src/app/core/rules/level-up.ts)) evaluates `13+` stat rules across **both existing classes and the incoming new class** whenever entering a brand-new class (allowing existing classes to continue leveling even if below `13`), returning all 13 `MulticlassOptionSchema` rows in `analyze_level_up` and rejecting ineligible multiclass attempts with `HTTP 400` in both `/forge/level-up-analysis` and `/forge/level-up-apply`. Entering a new class grants strictly PHB Level 1 multiclass armor, weapon (2014 vs 2024 Druid/Monk/Warlock parity), tool (*Artificer*/*Bard*/*Rogue*), and single bonus skill (*Bard*/*Ranger*/*Rogue*) proficiencies while preserving starting saving throw proficiencies unchanged.
* **Subclass Level Locks:** Enforces that 2024 classes (Cleric, Fighter, etc.) select subclasses at **Level 3**, whereas 2014 classes follow their respective 1st/2nd/3rd level rules.
* **Duplicate Non-Repeatable Feats:** [`deterministic_validate_build`](../../../backend/services/validation_service.py) flags and removes duplicate non-repeatable feat entries in `features_traits` and `advancements` (matching explicit `Feat:` / `Origin Feat:` / `source` feat entries cleanly via `resolve_known_feat` and `is_feat_repeatable` without false-positive substring collisions against class/subclass/racial features such as *Telekinetic Master*, *Blessed Healer*, *Undying Sentinel*, *Remarkable Athlete*, *Lucky*, or *Menacing*).
* **Point Buy & Array Boundaries:** Enforces base stat constraints (8 to 15 before racial/background bonuses).
* **Repo/Schema Guards:** [`RulesRepository`](../../../backend/repositories/rules_repository.py) enforces path confinement and returns deep copies (`copy.deepcopy`) on catalog getters so caller mutations cannot corrupt cached datasets; [`schemas.py`](../../../backend/core/schemas.py) (`sanitize_class_subclass_and_spells`) strips leading/trailing class whitespace and avoids `IndexError` or splitting multiclass/level strings into `subclass`.
